How Clarity thinks.

Seven questions. Each one makes the problem smaller.

  1. What's known. Things you could verify today.
  2. What's assumed. Things you're treating as true.
  3. What's unknown. Things that could change the picture.
  4. The Pivot. The one thing your decision actually depends on.
